The Phantom Troupe

A Dungeons & Dragons campaign against the Dragonborn cult

Welcome to the home of the Phantom Troupe, a band of misfit adventurers who met by chance in a crowded tavern and ended up as the biggest thorn in the side of a Dragonborn cult. The cult has taken over towns, filled its prisons with captured paladins, and answers to a mysterious leader known only as the Elder Dragon.

What started as a secret conversation over drinks turned into prison breaks, haunted throne rooms, a cursed forest, and a bloody night in the arena at the Festival of the Moon. Somewhere along the way, the newspapers started calling us heroes.

In Memory of Bjorn

Human Paladin · Founding Member of the Troupe

Bjorn was there from the very beginning. On the day the troupe first came together, it was Bjorn sitting across from Mogneuss at a tavern table, leaning in close and sharing secrets, never knowing Feitan was listening from the wall.

He was a giant of a man with a braided beard, a heavy warhammer, and a heart even bigger than his armor. He never once tried to be quiet, even when sneaking, and he never once backed down from a fight that mattered.

Bjorn fell during the prison break, cut down by the flying spikes of the cult superior Jameis while we fought to free Cassian and the captured paladins. He gave his life so that others could walk out of their cells. We won that fight for him, and we carry him with us on every road since.

"He held the line so we could keep walking."
